A Library of Dreams

A complete collection of my published works

The Elderwood Chronicles

Deep within an ancient forest that remembers the birth of stars, a young woman discovers she is the last guardian of a fading magic. She must unite the forest's feuding creatures against a creeping, silent darkness that threatens to consume all.

The Whispering Sea

A cartographer who has never seen the ocean is commissioned to map a mythical, ever-shifting sea. On her journey, she finds that the waves sing forgotten histories, and the constellations above are not what they seem. It's a tale of discovery, loss, and finding a home in the vast unknown.

The Starfall Key

In a city powered by fallen starlight, a gifted locksmith is drawn into a celestial conspiracy when she's hired to open a box that fell from the sky. What's inside holds the power to either reignite the dying stars or extinguish them forever.
